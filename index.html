About the event

What is the SEU Tech Event?

The SEU Tech Event is an annual gathering hosted by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southeast University. It brings together students, faculty members, and industry professionals to share knowledge on modern software development, artificial intelligence, and web technologies. The event is designed to give students practical exposure beyond the classroom, through live demonstrations and direct interaction with working engineers.

Our goal is simple: help students turn coursework into real, usable skills. Every session is built around a concrete outcome, whether that is deploying a first web application, understanding how a database scales, or learning how a hiring manager reads a portfolio. Attendance is free for all SEU students, and seats are limited, so early registration is encouraged.

Goals of the event

  • Introduce students to current industry practices in software engineering
  • Provide hands-on workshop time with real tools, not just slides
  • Create a space for students to showcase their own class and personal projects
  • Connect students with alumni and industry mentors for career guidance

Who can participate

  1. Undergraduate students of the Department of CSE
  2. Postgraduate students pursuing an MSc in CSE
  3. Students from other departments with an interest in technology
  4. Faculty members and invited guests
    1. Session speakers and panel moderators
    2. Workshop facilitators from partner companies

Workshops

Workshop tracks

Pick one workshop track during registration. Each track runs for two hours and includes a take-home project template.

Web & App Development

Build and deploy a small project using modern front-end tools.

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript fundamentals refresher
  • Connecting a front end to a simple API
  • Deploying a static site for free

AI & Data

An introduction to practical machine learning workflows.

  • Preparing and cleaning a dataset
  • Training a basic image classification model
  • Evaluating results and avoiding overfitting

Frequently asked questions

Is the event really free for SEU students?

Yes. Entry, session materials, and a certificate of participation are free for all currently enrolled SEU students.

Can I attend more than one workshop track?

Registration reserves one track per person so we can plan seating and materials, but you are welcome to sit in on a second track if seats remain available on the day.

What should I bring?

Bring your student ID card and a laptop if you plan to follow along with the hands-on workshops.
